Kuwait announces new limits on visa transfers amid expat crackdown

Kuwait’s Public Authority for Manpower announced a decision banning the visa transfer of government workers article (17) to the private sector article (18), and placing restrictions on transferring dependent visas to private sector work visas. The transfer ban of government workers article (17) to the private sector article (18) exempts:
- Kuwaiti women’s husbands and children.
- The wives of Kuwaitis.
- Palestinians with appropriate travel documents.
- Those health care workers such as doctors and nurses..

Expats over age 60 with no degree have until year's end to leave Kuwait

Expats in Kuwait over the age of 60 without a degree have until the end of the year 2020 to leave the country. This follows the news that Kuwait would stop issuing work permits for expats aged over 60 without a university degree, and will not extend any residencies and visas beyond August 31. Work permits for over 60s without a university degree will no longer be issued from January 1, 2021 and applies to all those who hold a higher secondary certificate or lower..
