How to request travel permit through SAHAL mobile App with procedure


1. The Procedure
* SAHAL app must be installed in your mobile from (play store for Android system and App Store for IOS). It will be identified by Kuwait Mobile ID. (need to install this app too).
* You can’t request the travel permit from another user SAHAl app.
* App availabe with Arabic and English version of SAHAL.
* This regulation started from 1/7/2025, all travelers can exit Kuwait before this date without travel permit.
* The Validation of the travel permit 7 days from the date of traveling the submitting date . example (if you’re traveling date on 5/7/2025, the end date will be after 7 days and should be 12/7/2025, system will reject if it exceeds 7 days duration).
* This validation is not related to how long you will spend outside Kuwait, it’s about the period will allow you to use the travel permit and exit Kuwait exp (you should exit Kuwait between 5/7/2025 and 12/7/2025) else you must submit anther request.
- Open SAHAL app.
- Goto Services tab.
- On the menu look for Public Authority of manpower
- Through this clicks to drop down the menu look for Employees Services Click on Issuing travel permit
- On the form enter the traveling date as per the format mentioned above in Procedure tab and click the blue button to proceed the request,
- confirmation page will be showing up with request number, see the image.
- Wait until this request will be approved.
- Travel permit will be sent to your SAHAL app.
- Print the permit and keep it along with your ticket for airport review.

2- How to submit the request
• Open SAHAL app and be sure you’re using the Arabic version.
• Go to Services tab (خدمات)
• On the menu look for Public Authority of manpower (الهيئة العامة للقوى العاملة)
• Though this clicks to drop down the menu look for Employees Services (خدمات العمالة الوافدة)
• Click on Issuing travel permit (إصدار إذن نغادرة)
• On the form enter the traveling date as per the format mentioned above in Procedure tab and click the blue button to proceed the request, confirmation page will be showing up with request number, see the image >>.
• Wait until this request will be approved.
• Travel permit will be sent to your SAHAL app.
• Print the permit and keep it along with your ticket for airport review.

3. How to download the travel permit from SAHAL app after approval
1- Open SAHAL app (Arabic version only)
2- Goto Services tab
3- On the menu look for Public Authority of manpower Through this click to drop down the menu look for Certificate Services
4- Click on Download Certificate
From the drop down the menu selects the printing Public Authority of manpower certificate.
Download the certificate and print it for use in airport.

Kuwait’s new travel rule: Expat Kids need Father’s Approval to Leave the Country. on 4/8/2024


*** Expat Kids under 18 accompanied by their mother or a relative without the father present during the trip need Father’s Approval to Leave the Country. ***

Explenation.
Ministry of interior (MOI) in Airport has begun implementing a new system that prevents any expatriate child from leaving the country without the approval of their guardian. This requires the guardian to sign a statement prepared by the Passports Department at Ministry of interior in the Airport. The decision applies only to departing expatriates, even if the child is accompanied by their mother or a relative without the father present during the trip. The regulation, now in effect at all Kuwait ports, requires that expatriate children receive explicit approval and a signature from their father before being allowed to leave the country. Under the new rule, if a father legally sponsors his child within the country, his consent is mandatory for the child to travel with the mother.

Education Certificate Authentication in PAM system required now for Residency process(Renewal – Local Transfer).


*** Residency process will be onhold till the Education Certificate Authentication in PAM system for high titles ***

Explenation.
Please note that most of the professions have been canceled from the old Ministry’s system in the Public Authority for Manpower, as the professions must be changed as per the list of uniform GCC titles. Every new job title that will be changed must have an educational qualification that matches the new title. Every employee is requested to verify the academic qualification in the work permit, and whoever has an updated academic qualification must send it before starting any procedure (transfer - renewal) to approve the practical qualification in the work permit, whether university, secondary, or intermediate. There is an attachment for the documents required to approve the practical qualification in the work permit. Academic qualification email. The academic qualification cannot be updated except when the residency renewal or transfer is about to occur.
Documents required to approve the university qualification for holders of a bachelor’s degree:
• Original certified university degree (bachelor’s degree) + original certified translation.
• A copy of the complete four-year transcript + translation.
• A copy of the high school certificate + translation (for Indian nationality, a copy of the high school certificate is required for grades 10-11-12)
Documents required to approve the university qualification if the certificate is higher than a bachelor’s degree:
• University degree (Bachelor’s + Master’s) original, certified + original certified translation.
• A copy of the full four-year transcript (Bachelor’s + Master’s) + translation.
• A copy of the high school certificate + translation (for Indian nationality, a copy of the high school certificate is required for grades 10-11-12)
Documents required for secondary and intermediate qualifications:
A copy of your secondary or intermediate certificate, translated.
Note :
Please send the qualification update documents three months before the end of the residency to amend the qualification and then send the transfer or renewal transaction.
• University degrees in engineering. A certificate from the Kuwait Society of Engineers is required.
