Residency - NOC - CVV process requirements

Click the options below to view more details.

Comiricial Visit Visa - CVV
* Clear passport copy with validity not less than 6 months,
* Degree copy or GCC residency with high title, for asian + arab national
* Fill the CVV form and sign it from HR Manager & V.P with printed name.
No Objection Certificate - NOC
* Clear passport copy with validity not less than 2 years.
* Degree copy for high title.
* Driving licenses (DL) copy for drivers.
* Fill the NOC form and sign it from HR Manager & Director with printed name.
Residency Renewal
* Clear passport copy with validity not less than 1 year.
* CivilID copy.
* Degree copy for high title.
* Driving licenses (DL) copy for drivers.
* Submit the request through TalentHub and send the above requirements by email to ES.
First Time Residency
* Original NOC visa + 3 copies front and back of it.
* 2 Clear passport copy with entry stamp page and last page if needed.
* Original PCC.
* Original Degree.
* Driving licenses (DL) copy for drivers.
* Fill the NOC conversion form and sign it from HR Manager with printed name.
Local Transfer Residency
- Residency transfer request with title and salary signed by HR Manager. Furthermore, there must be a cooperation between ES employee and HR regarding title selection.
* Passport copy.
* Civil ID copy.
* Residency sequence report for MOl (if needed).
* Degree copy attested and translated (if needed).
* Kuwaiti driving license copy (if needed).
* Work Permit and Authorized Signatory are compulsory.
- Upon employee joining, recruitment team must send ES the following to start the residency transfer process by email:
* Transfer documents signed by ex-employer by blue pen. (Article 17 and 22).
* End of Service Letter from provided ex-sponsor. (Article 17).
* Signed Work Contract with blue pen in PDF and colored format. (Article 18, 17 and 22).
* MOI Form signed by ex-sponsor (Article 17 and 22); MOI form signed by current sponsor (Article 18).
* Work permit copy. (If applicable) (Article 18).
* Passport and residency copy. (Article 18, 17 and 22).
* Civil ID Copy. (Article 18, 17 and 22).
* PF number. (Article 18, 17 and 22).
* Copy driving license if (driver). (Article 18, 17 and 22).
* Copy degree (Kuwait embassy in the country of origin, Kuwait ministry of foreign affairs, translation to Arabic and attestation from Kuwait ministry of justice) if applicable. (Article 18, 17 and 22).

Note: Above requirments subject to change any time as per Kuwait authority roles.
